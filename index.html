About Me

I'm a non-binary Indigenous game developer based in Winnipeg, MB, Canada. I specialize in Unity, with a focus on gameplay programming, VR/AR/XR, and technical direction. I've shipped VR titles, multiplayer Quest projects, mobile apps and web games, often as lead or sole programmer, and I own features from concept and pitch through to final delivery. I'm passionate about creating emotionally rich and mechanically engaging experiences.
